MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT  
(Working draft for counsel review — not for signature)

This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made as of the Effective Date \[TO CONFIRM\] between Orion Analytics Ltd. (“Supplier”) and Northstar Retail Inc. (“Customer”). Addresses and notice emails: \[TO CONFIRM\].

This is a working draft only. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before signing. Data protection, insurance, export control and similar topics are not agreed and are not stated as agreed.

1. Definitions  
1.1 “SOW” means a signed Statement of Work describing data analytics configuration and support.  
1.2 “Deliverables” means items specifically commissioned under an SOW.  
1.3 “Background IP” means IP owned or licensed by a party independently of this Agreement.  
1.4 “Confidential Information” has the meaning in Section 8.  
1.5 Other capitalized terms have the meanings given in the term sheet or the Section where first used.

2. Structure and precedence  
Services are provided under this Agreement and signed SOWs. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement on commercial details (fees, timeline, scope) the SOW governs for that engagement; this Agreement governs all other terms. No SOW amends Sections 7–13 unless the SOW expressly identifies the Section.

3. Services  
Supplier shall perform data analytics configuration and support as described in signed SOWs. Supplier shall perform in a professional manner consistent with Section 9.

4. Term and renewal  
The term is two (2) years from the Effective Date \[TO CONFIRM\], then automatic successive one-year renewals unless either party gives at least sixty (60) days’ notice of non-renewal before the then-current term ends.

5. SOWs and change control  
5.1 Each SOW shall set fees, scope and timeline.  
5.2 Changes to scope, timeline or fees require a written Change Order signed by both parties. Verbal instructions do not amend an SOW.

6. Fees and payment  
6.1 Fees are as stated in each SOW.  
6.2 Invoices are issued monthly in arrears. Amounts are due thirty (30) days after invoice date.  
6.3 Disputed amounts must be raised within fifteen (15) days of invoice date. Timely disputed amounts may be withheld pending resolution; undisputed amounts remain payable.  
6.4 Taxes, late-interest rate and invoice currency: \[TO CONFIRM\].

7. Intellectual property  
7.1 Each party retains its Background IP.  
7.2 Upon full payment of the applicable SOW fees, Customer owns specifically commissioned Deliverables.  
7.3 Supplier retains general tools, templates and know-how. To the extent Deliverables embed Supplier Background IP, Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, internal-use license to that embedded Background IP.  
7.4 Except as stated, neither party assigns residual know-how that is not a Deliverable.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Mutual. Each party shall protect the other party’s Confidential Information for three (3) years after disclosure; trade secrets shall be protected while legally protected as trade secrets.  
8.2 Use is limited to performing this Agreement.  
8.3 Standard exclusions (public, previously known, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party) apply. Compelled disclosure: \[TO CONFIRM process\].  
8.4 Definition of Confidential Information: \[TO CONFIRM if marking required\].

9. Warranty  
9.1 Supplier warrants that services will be performed professionally.  
9.2 Customer’s exclusive remedy for breach of this Section 9 is re-performance, provided Customer notifies Supplier within thirty (30) days after the non-conforming services were performed.  
9.3 No other warranty is agreed in the term sheet. Fitness, merchantability and error-free operation are not stated as agreed.

10. Indemnity  
10.1 Supplier shall defend Customer against third-party claims that Deliverables infringe IP, and pay finally awarded damages, subject to Section 11.  
10.2 Exclusions: Customer modifications; combinations not supplied by Supplier; use outside documentation.  
10.3 Customer shall give prompt notice and reasonable cooperation; Supplier shall have sole control of the defense, provided any settlement that imposes an obligation on Customer other than cessation of use of the alleged infringing Deliverable requires Customer’s consent (not to be unreasonably withheld). \[Control mechanics not in term sheet — flagged as drafting, not a new commercial bargain; counsel to confirm.\]  
10.4 No Customer indemnity was agreed.

11. Liability  
11.1 Each party’s aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.  
11.2 The cap does not apply to: fraud; wilful misconduct; confidentiality breach; or IP infringement indemnity under Section 10.  
11.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, except amounts payable under an indemnity.  
11.4 Nothing in this Section excludes liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law \[TO CONFIRM counsel\].

12. Termination  
12.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or an affected SOW for material breach if the breach remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice.  
12.2 Either party may terminate immediately for insolvency.  
12.3 Customer may terminate an SOW for convenience with thirty (30) days’ notice, and shall pay committed fees plus non-cancellable costs.  
12.4 Survival: Sections 7, 8, 10, 11, 13 and accrued payment obligations survive.

13. Governing law and disputes  
13.1 Singapore law.  
13.2 Disputes shall be escalated to the parties’ executives for fifteen (15) days.  
13.3 Thereafter, disputes shall be finally resolved by SIAC arbitration in Singapore, English language, one arbitrator.

14. Notices  
Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing to the emails/addresses \[TO CONFIRM\].

15. General  
15.1 Neither party may assign without consent except to an affiliate or successor to substantially all assets, with notice. \[TO CONFIRM — not in term sheet; alternative is to mark assignment as \[TO CONFIRM\] only.\] Assignment is \[TO CONFIRM\].  
15.2 This Agreement and signed SOWs are the entire agreement on the subject matter.  
15.3 Amendments must be in writing and signed.  
15.4 No third-party beneficiaries except as required for the Section 10 defense.  
15.5 Counterparts and electronic signatures permitted \[TO CONFIRM\].

16. Records and audit of fees  
Supplier shall keep reasonable records of time and materials or milestone evidence required to support invoices under each SOW for twelve (12) months after the SOW ends. Customer may inspect those fee records on reasonable notice, not more than once per calendar year, during business hours, limited to records needed to verify amounts invoiced. This Section does not create a security audit right, a data-protection audit, or any insurance certificate right; those topics remain \[TO CONFIRM\] and are not agreed.

17. Non-solicitation \[TO CONFIRM — not in term sheet\]  
The term sheet is silent. This draft therefore does not impose a non-solicitation covenant. If the parties later agree one, it must be added by signed amendment.

18. Force majeure \[TO CONFIRM — not in term sheet\]  
The term sheet is silent. No force majeure excuse is granted in this working draft. Counsel may add a balanced clause; until then, delay is handled only through the written Change Order process in Section 5.2 and the termination rights in Section 12.

19. Relationship of the parties  
The parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship. Neither party may bind the other except as a signed SOW or Change Order expressly authorises. Supplier personnel assigned to an SOW remain Supplier’s employees or contractors.

20. Order of performance and cooperation  
Customer shall provide timely access, data, decisions and reasonably requested information described in the applicable SOW. If Customer delay materially prevents performance, Supplier shall give written notice and the parties shall use a Change Order to adjust timeline or fees. This cooperation sentence implements performance mechanics; it does not add a new fee type beyond the SOW and Change Order rules already agreed.

21. Publicity \[TO CONFIRM\]  
Neither party shall issue a press release using the other party’s name without prior written consent. The term sheet did not agree a publicity right.

22. Export, insurance, data protection (placeholder only)  
The parties have not agreed a data protection addendum, insurance limits, or export-control allocation. Those subjects are listed in Section 23 and must not be read into Sections 3, 8, 10 or 11 by implication. If Customer requires processing of personal data, the parties shall negotiate a separate addendum before such processing starts. No processing role (controller/processor) is assigned in this draft.

23. Not agreed — excluded from operative promises  
The following are not agreed and are not granted or waived by this draft: data protection addendum / GDPR / PDPA terms; insurance limits or certificates; export controls; security exhibit beyond what an SOW may later add; Effective Date; addresses; notice emails.

Customer owns specifically commissioned Deliverables after full payment, as already stated in Section 7.2. Supplier’s retention of tools, templates and know-how is not a licence to reuse Customer Confidential Information. If a Deliverable includes third-party components, Supplier shall identify those components in the SOW; licence terms for third-party components are \[TO CONFIRM\] if not stated in the SOW. Acceptance, if any, shall follow the SOW; this Agreement does not add an extra acceptance period beyond the thirty-day re-performance notice in Section 9.2.
