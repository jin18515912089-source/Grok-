> 本文件为合同审阅意见，不构成正式法律意见。仅基于题内文本，不声称适用于所有司法辖区。

## 二、建议英文修订条款（相互一致、双向对等）

**1. Definition of Confidential Information.**
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the parties' product integration evaluation, whether in written, oral, or electronic form, and which is marked as confidential or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

**2. Obligations.**
The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using reasonable care, and may disclose it only to its employees, affiliates, and professional advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set out herein. The obligations in this section shall survive for three (3) years from the date of disclosure; provided that, to the extent any Confidential Information constitutes a trade secret, such obligations shall survive for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

**3. Residuals.**
Neither party shall be liable for the use by its personnel of general ideas, concepts, know-how, or techniques retained in the unaided memory of such personnel as a result of access to Confidential Information; provided that this clause does not grant any license under any party's intellectual property rights and does not relieve either party of its confidentiality obligations with respect to specific Confidential Information.

**4. Equitable Relief.**
Each party acknowledges that a breach of the confidentiality obligations may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, either party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief without the necessity of proving actual harm.

**5. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution.**
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [State/Country TO CONFIRM]. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration conducted in a neutral location agreed by the parties, in the English language.

**6. Exclusions.**
Confidential Information shall not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without an obligation of confidentiality; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without an obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**一致性说明：** 修订条款已将原单向定义、单方剩余信息权、单方禁令与单方管辖改为双向对等；保密期三年、商业秘密持续保护、允许关联公司/专业顾问、中立仲裁均与商业背景一致。
