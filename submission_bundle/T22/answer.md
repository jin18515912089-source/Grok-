Supplier and Customer are each referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND EFFECTIVE DATE

1.1 **Effective Date** means the date of mutual execution of this Agreement **[TO CONFIRM]**.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the applicable Statement of Work.

---

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Services.** Supplier shall provide data analytics configuration and support services as described in each signed Statement of Work ("**SOW**"). Each SOW is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** The scope, timeline, fees, and deliverables for each engagement shall be set out in a signed SOW.

2.3 **Change Control.** Any change to the scope, timeline, or fees of a SOW shall require a written Change Order signed by both Parties.

---

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for two (2) years (the "**Initial Term**").

3.2 **Renewal.** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives the other at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term.

3.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within such thirty (30) day period.

3.4 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases to conduct business in the ordinary course.

3.5 **Termination for Convenience (Customer).** Customer may terminate an individual SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice, and shall pay Supplier all committed fees plus non-cancellable costs incurred in connection with such SOW.

---

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Fees for each SOW shall be as stated in that SOW.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer shall raise any disputed amount in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the applicable invoice; amounts not disputed within such period shall be deemed accepted.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 **Background IP.** Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in and to its pre-existing intellectual property and any intellectual property developed independently of this Agreement ("**Background IP**").

5.2 **Deliverables.** Customer shall own all specifically commissioned Deliverables (as defined in the applicable SOW) upon full payment of all amounts due for such Deliverables.

5.3 **Supplier Tools.** Supplier retains all general tools, templates, and know-how, whether pre-existing or developed in the course of performing the Services.

5.4 **License.** To the extent any Deliverable embeds Supplier Background IP, Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, internal-use license to use such embedded Supplier Background IP solely in connection with the Deliverables.

---

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 **Obligations.** The Parties shall protect each other's confidential information using reasonable care.

6.2 **Term.** Confidentiality obligations shall continue for three (3) years after disclosure; provided that trade secrets shall remain protected for so long as they remain legally protected.

---

## 7. WARRANTY

7.1 **Performance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

7.2 **Exclusive Remedy.** Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty shall be re-performance of the non-conforming Services, provided Customer notifies Supplier in writing within thirty (30) days of the non-conforming performance.

---

## 8. LIABILITY

8.1 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to liability.

8.2 **Exclusions from Cap.** The limitation in Section 8.1 shall not apply to: (a) fraud; (b) wilful misconduct; (c) breach of confidentiality; or (d) the IP infringement indemnity in Section 9.

8.3 **Consequential Loss.** Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, except for amounts payable under an indemnity.

---

## 9. INDEMNITY

9.1 **IP Infringement.** Supplier shall defend Customer against any third-party claim that the Deliverables infringe the intellectual property rights of such third party, and shall indemnify Customer for amounts finally awarded against Customer in connection with such claim.

9.2 **Exclusions.** Supplier shall have no obligation under Section 9.1 to the extent the claim arises from: (a) modifications to the Deliverables made by or on behalf of Customer; (b) combinations of the Deliverables with items not supplied by Supplier; or (c) use of the Deliverables outside the applicable documentation.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Singapore.

11.3 **Arbitration.** Any dispute not resolved under Section 11.2 shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in Singapore, in the English language, by a single arbitrator.
