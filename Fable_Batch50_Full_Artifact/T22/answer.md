# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (WORKING DRAFT)

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date – TO CONFIRM] (the "Effective Date") between **Orion Analytics Ltd.**, a company with its registered address at [TO CONFIRM] ("Supplier"), and **Northstar Retail Inc.**, a company with its registered address at [TO CONFIRM] ("Customer"). Supplier and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Definitions
1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
1.2 "Background IP" means all intellectual property rights owned or controlled by a Party before the Effective Date or developed independently of this Agreement.
1.3 "Change Order" means a written document signed by both Parties that modifies the scope, timeline or fees of an SOW in accordance with Section 4.
1.4 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.
1.5 "Deliverables" means the work product specifically commissioned by Customer and identified as a deliverable in an SOW.
1.6 "Services" means the data analytics configuration and support services described in a signed SOW.
1.7 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document signed by both Parties describing Services, Deliverables, fees, timelines and acceptance criteria, which incorporates this Agreement by reference.
1.8 "Supplier Tools" means Supplier's general tools, templates, methodologies and know-how, including improvements thereto, that are not specifically commissioned Deliverables.

## 2. Structure and Statements of Work
2.1 This Agreement sets out the terms governing all Services. Each engagement shall be described in an SOW. No SOW is binding until signed by both Parties.
2.2 In the event of conflict, the following order of precedence applies: (a) a Change Order, for the matter it addresses; (b) the SOW; (c) this Agreement; except that an SOW may not modify Sections 9 (Liability), 10 (Indemnity) or 13 (Governing Law and Dispute Resolution) unless it expressly states the Section being modified.
2.3 Supplier shall perform the Services in accordance with the applicable SOW and Section 8 (Warranty).

## 3. Term and Renewal
3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of two (2) years (the "Initial Term").
3.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
3.3 Expiry or termination of this Agreement does not by itself terminate any SOW then in effect; this Agreement continues to govern such SOW until it is completed or terminated under Section 11.

## 4. Change Control
4.1 Any change to the scope, timeline or fees of an SOW requires a Change Order signed by both Parties.
4.2 Either Party may request a change in writing. Supplier shall respond with an assessment of impact on scope, timeline and fees within a reasonable period. Neither Party is obliged to agree to a change, and Supplier is not required to perform changed work until a Change Order is signed.

## 5. Fees, Invoicing and Payment
5.1 Customer shall pay the fees stated in each SOW.
5.2 Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall reference the applicable SOW and describe the Services performed.
5.3 Undisputed amounts are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
5.4 If Customer disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, Customer must notify Supplier in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, describing the basis of the dispute. Amounts not disputed within that period are deemed accepted. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve disputed amounts promptly, and Customer shall pay any undisputed portion when due.

## 6. Intellectual Property
6.1 **Background IP.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of either Party's Background IP.
6.2 **Deliverables.** Subject to Section 6.3 and upon Customer's payment in full of the fees payable for the relevant Deliverable, Supplier assigns to Customer all right, title and interest in that specifically commissioned Deliverable. Until full payment, Customer's right to use the Deliverable is a limited, revocable license for the Purpose set out in the SOW.
6.3 **Supplier Tools.** Supplier retains all right, title and interest in Supplier Tools and other Supplier Background IP, whether or not incorporated into Deliverables. To the extent any Supplier Background IP is embedded in a Deliverable, Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable (except to a successor of the Customer business) license to use such embedded Supplier Background IP solely as part of the Deliverable for Customer's internal business purposes.
6.4 **No Other Rights.** Except as expressly stated, no license or other right is granted by either Party by implication or otherwise.

## 7. Confidentiality
7.1 Each Party (as recipient) shall (a) use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services and exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) protect it with no less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 7.
7.2 Confidential Information excludes information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient; (b) was known to the recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the discloser's Confidential Information.
7.3 A recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or court order, provided it gives prompt notice (where legally permitted) and discloses only what is required.
7.4 The obligations in this Section 7 continue for three (3) years after the date of disclosure of the relevant information; provided that for information constituting a trade secret under applicable law, the obligations continue for so long as it remains legally protected as a trade secret.
7.5 On termination or written request, the recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information, except for copies in routine backups or retained as required by law, which remain subject to this Section 7.

## 8. Warranty and Exclusive Remedy
8.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards.
8.2 If Customer notifies Supplier in writing within thirty (30) days after performance of any Services that do not conform to Section 8.1, describing the non-conformity in reasonable detail, Supplier shall re-perform the non-conforming Services at no additional charge. Re-performance is Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in Section 8.1.
8.3 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, Supplier disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, to the maximum extent permitted by law. [TO CONFIRM with counsel: scope of disclaimer under Singapore law.]

## 9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 **Aggregate Cap.** Subject to Section 9.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement and all SOWs, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
9.2 **Exclusion of Indirect Loss.** Subject to Section 9.3, neither Party shall be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, revenue, business or anticipated savings, except that amounts payable under an indemnity in Section 10 shall not be treated as excluded indirect or consequential loss.
9.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 9.1 and 9.2 do not apply to (a) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (b) wilful misconduct; (c) breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality); or (d) Supplier's indemnity obligations under Section 10 for intellectual property infringement.

## 10. Intellectual Property Indemnity
10.1 Supplier shall defend Customer against any claim by a third party alleging that a Deliverable, as delivered by Supplier and used in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable documentation, infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or settlement amounts agreed by Supplier.
10.2 Supplier has no obligation under Section 10.1 to the extent a claim arises from (a) modifications to the Deliverable made by or for Customer other than by Supplier; (b) combination of the Deliverable with products, data or materials not supplied by Supplier, where the claim would not have arisen but for such combination; or (c) use of the Deliverable outside the scope of the documentation or this Agreement.
10.3 Customer shall (a) notify Supplier promptly in writing of the claim; (b) give Supplier sole control of the defence and settlement, provided Supplier shall not settle any claim in a manner that admits fault on behalf of Customer or imposes obligations on Customer without Customer's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld); and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at Supplier's expense.
10.4 If a Deliverable is, or in Supplier's reasonable opinion is likely to be, held infringing, Supplier may at its option and expense (a) procure the right for Customer to continue using it; (b) modify or replace it so that it is non-infringing without material loss of functionality; or (c) if neither is commercially reasonable, terminate the affected SOW and refund fees paid for the affected Deliverable, less a reasonable amount for use to date. ## 11. Termination
11.1 **Material Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any affected SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or the SOW and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach.
11.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver or administrator appointed, or is subject to any analogous event.
11.3 **Convenience (SOW).** Customer may terminate any SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. In that case Customer shall pay (a) all fees for Services performed and Deliverables delivered up to the effective date of termination; (b) all committed fees stated in the SOW as non-cancellable or minimum commitments; and (c) Supplier's reasonable, documented non-cancellable costs incurred in reliance on the SOW.
11.4 **Effect.** On termination, each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information under Section 7.5, Supplier shall deliver to Customer all Deliverables (complete or in progress) for which Customer has paid, and Customer shall pay all amounts due. Sections 1, 5 (for accrued amounts), 6, 7, 8.3, 9, 10, 11.4, 12 and 13 survive termination.

## 12. General
12.1 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.
12.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except to an Affiliate or a successor to substantially all of its relevant business, with notice to the other Party.
12.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses and notice emails stated below or as updated by notice. [Addresses and notice emails – TO CONFIRM.]
12.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate. Payment obligations are not excused. [TO CONFIRM: not addressed in the term sheet.]
12.5 **Entire Agreement; Amendment.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs and Change Orders, is the entire agreement on its subject matter and supersedes prior agreements. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
12.6 **Severability; Waiver.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. A waiver is effective only if in writing and does not constitute a waiver of any other right.
12.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## 13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
13.2 **Escalation.** Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be referred in writing to senior executives of each Party, who shall meet and attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) days of the referral.
13.3 **Arbitration.** If the dispute is not resolved within that period, it shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its rules in force at the time of the notice of arbitration. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore, the language shall be English, and the tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. Nothing prevents either Party from seeking interim or injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.
